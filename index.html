In una riga

I tuoi dati di gioco non stanno su un server nostro, perché non abbiamo un server. Partite, punteggi, obiettivi, serie e impostazioni vivono sul tuo telefono e non vengono inviati a noi. Lumenar non ha account, non chiede email, non chiede password. Le uniche cose che escono dal dispositivo sono quelle tecniche necessarie a mostrare gli annunci, misurare in forma anonima come funziona l'app e segnalarci gli errori.

9. Minori

Lumenar non è destinato a minori di 14 anni, età minima per esprimere il consenso ai servizi della società dell'informazione in Italia. In altri Paesi dell'Unione l'età minima può essere diversa (tra 13 e 16 anni): l'app non è destinata a chi non ha l'età minima prevista nel proprio Paese. Se veniamo a conoscenza di dati raccolti da un minore al di sotto di tale età, li cancelliamo.

10. Sicurezza

I dati che escono dal dispositivo sono trasmessi cifrati (HTTPS/TLS). I dati di gioco restano nella memoria privata dell'app, a cui altre applicazioni non accedono. Nessun sistema è infallibile: in caso di violazione dei dati che presenti un rischio elevato per i tuoi diritti, ti informeremo come richiesto dall'art. 34 GDPR.

11. Modifiche a questa informativa

Se modifichiamo l'informativa in modo sostanziale, aggiorneremo la data in cima e te ne daremo avviso nell'app. Le versioni precedenti restano disponibili su richiesta.

Privacy Policy — Lumenar

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Version 3.0

In one line

Your game data is not on a server of ours, because we do not have a server. Games, scores, achievements, streaks and settings live on your phone and are never sent to us. Lumenar has no accounts, asks for no email and no password. The only things that leave your device are the technical ones needed to show ads, measure anonymously how the app is used, and tell us about crashes.

1. Who processes your data

The controller is Tommaso Lucchesi, an individual, [ADDRESS], Italy. For any question about this policy or your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What stays on your phone

The following is never sent to us or to anyone else. It lives in the app's storage on your device, and you can export, restore or delete it at any time (§7): games played with score, duration and difficulty; level, XP, lives, medals and unlocked achievements; daily streak and the name you chose; your settings, including notification preferences. We do not ask for an email and we do not ask for a password, because there is no account. There is no server-side profile, no sign-up, no login.

3. What leaves the device

Advertising (advertising identifier, IP address, approximate device data, ad interactions) — to serve the ads that keep the app free, and with your consent to personalise them. Usage analytics (which screens you open, which games you start, anonymous usage events) — to understand which features matter. Diagnostics (crash and error reports, device model, OS and app version) — to know when the app breaks. Purchases (if you subscribe, the purchase provider receives an anonymous device identifier and the purchase data) — to deliver and restore what you bought. We never receive your card or payment details: payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Legal bases: your consent for personalised advertising; legitimate interest for non-personalised ads, analytics and diagnostics; performance of a contract for purchases.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use these processors, acting on our behalf: Google (AdMob — ad serving and consent management; Google Play — distribution and payments), PostHog Inc. (usage analytics), Sentry / Functional Software, Inc. (crash and error reporting), RevenueCat Inc. (subscriptions and in-app purchases). We may also disclose data to judicial or administrative authorities where required by law.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, where the provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep it

Game data on your device: until you delete it from the app. Analytics and diagnostics: [TO BE CONFIRMED], depending on the retention configured with each provider. Billing and tax data: for the period required by Italian accounting and tax law. Advertising identifier: handled by Google; once you withdraw consent it is no longer used to personalise ads.

7. Your rights, and how to exercise them in the app

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. Three of these you can exercise directly, without writing to us: Settings → Privacy & Data → Export (access and portability: get a file with all your data); Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all data (erasure); Settings → Ad privacy options (withdrawing ad consent). For anything else, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within one month. You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

8. Advertising: consent and withdrawal

Before the first ad we ask for consent to personalised advertising. If you decline, you still see non-personalised ads and the app stays free: the only difference is that the advertising identifier is not used to profile you. You can change your mind any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.

9. Minors

Lumenar is not intended for children under 14, the minimum age for consenting to information society services in Italy. In other EU countries the minimum age may differ (between 13 and 16): the app is not intended for anyone below the minimum age applicable in their country. If we learn we have collected data from a child below that age, we delete it.

10. Security

Data leaving the device is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Game data stays in the app's private storage, which other applications cannot access. No system is infallible: in the event of a data breach posing a high risk to your rights, we will inform you as required by Art. 34 GDPR.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app. Previous versions remain available on request.
